After listening to a true-crime podcast with my partner about the [Elliot Rodger case](https://open.spotify.com/episode/3xOWLwAM71xE1rcPAUxjmd) - the guy who became enraged over women rejecting him and eventually killed 6 people and injured 14 others - I decided to dive a little deeper into the whole incel phenomenon.

So I spent about an hour going through their most popular forum.

**And what I found was BAD.**

Like every young person, I had frustrations with the dating market growing up. In my case, at around 18 I remember being annoyed that I was competing with 30- and 40-year-old men for the same girls.

I guess now it’s even worse because once you hit 12 you’re competing with American politicians... But that’s a different story.

What surprised me on these forums was how many guys had taken normal frustrations around dating and turned them into an entire worldview. There are guys openly talking about violence because women won’t sleep with them.

They’ll quote studies about how important affection and physical touch are to human wellbeing, then somehow make the leap to:

> “If women won’t hug me, nobody should be getting hugged.”

They obsess over race, height, facial structure, jawlines, income and basically every characteristic imaginable. A lot of them seem obsessed specifically with “thin hot white women” - while simultaneously referring to them as “foids” and “toilets.”

Which makes me ask the question: "So...Like...Do they want women or not?"

---

They’re terrified of approaching women, yet angry that women, the physically more vulnerable sex, aren’t approaching them. It’s an entire ecosystem of men feeding each other hypothetical traumatic situations and then finding studies, statistics and anecdotes that confirm the worldview they already have.

I saw one guy write:

> “I pay my taxes and contribute to society, a foid should be served to me on a silver platter.”

Another described men as basically:

> “Grindmaxxing for Chad’s leftovers.”

---

There was one thread that particularly interested me. It was about Tinder potentially allowing women to filter men based on height, which I think was an attention play by Tinder's marketing team, but nonetheless.

Height comes up CONSTANTLY in these communities.

And it stood out to me because I’m very tall, so obviously I know height can be attractive to some women. But at the same time, some of the biggest players I’ve personally known have been like 5'6"–5'10".

---

The older I get, the more I find myself agreeing with one idea from Adlerian psychology:

People often decide how they want to live first, and then collect evidence that justifies living that way.

“This girl rejected me when I was 9, therefore I’ll never approach women again.”

Maybe.

Or maybe approaching women is uncomfortable, rejection hurts, and believing the entire dating market is rigged means you never have to risk finding out whether someone actually likes you.

You see the same mentality everywhere.

“The system is corrupt, so why bother working?”

“The dating market is rigged, so why bother talking to women?”

**A worldview can very easily become permission to do nothing.**

But it can also give you someone else to blame for the life you’re not living.

And that’s where the incel ideology becomes dangerous. Because once you convince yourself that women aren’t simply rejecting you, but actively depriving you of something you’re entitled to, resentment can very quickly turn into the belief that they deserve to be punished.

Being lonely, sexually frustrated or rejected is not unusual. Almost every man or woman experiences some version of that at some age.

But if thousands of frustrated men sit together every day convincing each other that women are responsible for their suffering, eventually some of them start believing they need to seek "[retribution](https://www.nytimes.com/video/us/100000002900707/youtube-video-retribution.html)."

And regardless of whatever frustrations you might currently have with women, remember: one day you might have a daughter. Your best friend might have one. Your brother might have one.

And she’ll eventually enter the dating market - filled with men who have convinced themselves that rejection isn't simply disappointing, but an injustice being committed against them.

And that’s when all the memes and clips you come across today will no longer be funny.
